DCOM 3002 · Design Experiment

Attar Personalizer App

UX / UI Design Figma Adobe Illustrator App Design Moodboarding

The physical object chosen was Attar (also called Ittar) — an alcohol-free, oil-based perfume rooted in Middle Eastern and South Asian culture, crafted from aromatic ingredients like oud, rose, jasmine, saffron, and sandalwood.

The digital concept translates the personal, sensory experience of blending an Attar into an interactive app. Users start with an empty vial and build their own scent by selecting from three ingredient categories — Floral, Spice, and Wood — each with adjustable sliders to control the concentration of individual notes. Since a screen can't convey smell, competing sensory descriptors at the bottom of the app (Spicy ↔ Sweet, Cool ↔ Warm, Floral ↔ Woody) visualise the balance of the blend instead.

The design went through several iterations — an initial attempt at vibe-coding a prototype produced an unintended 8-bit aesthetic, which prompted a return to Figma. The final mockup features a carved-wood visual language with iconography designed in Illustrator, a clear navigation bar, and familiar UX conventions like a 3-dot settings menu to keep the interface intuitive.

Design Process
01 — Visual Moodboard
Ingredients, cultural references, and initial colour palette exploring the world of Attar.
02 — Idea Map
Concept map tracing Attar from its aromatic ingredients through tactile, cultural, and emotional associations — informing the app's three ingredient categories.
03 — Refined Moodboard
Second moodboard developed after returning to the original concept — focusing on a warm wood aesthetic with Oud and Agarwood as the central theme.
04 — Wireframe
Wireframe establishing the navigation bar, ingredient selector panel, central Attar display, and sensory bar graph at the bottom.
05 — Final Mockup
Final Figma mockup showing the Wood ingredient screen. The user has just adjusted the Agarwood/Oud slider to 80% — the bottle's fill colour reflects the dominant note. Swiping left or right cycles between Floral, Spice, and Wood categories to continue building the blend.

DCOM 3900 · December 2025

Synergy Digital Business Plan

Business Planning Financial Strategy Brand Development Market Analysis Freelance Strategy

A full business plan for Synergy Digital — a specialized freelance digital communications service focused exclusively on the financial sector in the Greater Toronto Area. The concept translates my real-world experience working with IG Wealth Management advisors into a scalable business model built around compliance-first content creation and financial thought leadership.

The plan covers three core services: a Thought Leadership Retainer (tiered at $3,500–$8,000/month), a Compliance-Focused Content Audit & Setup, and Investor Education Campaigns. The value proposition is specific — unlike generalist agencies, Synergy Digital integrates regulatory adherence (CIRO/OSC standards) directly into the content creation process, reducing compliance risk for financial advisors and wealth managers while building their digital authority.

The plan includes a full financial management section with startup cost projections, a 12-month income statement, a 3-month cash flow forecast, a sample invoice, and a market and competitive analysis identifying three competitor segments: generalist agencies, niche compliance consultants, and internal staff — and articulating how Synergy Digital outperforms each.
