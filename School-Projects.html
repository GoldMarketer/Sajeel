DCOM 3002 · Interactive Design

Attar Personalizer - A Concept App

UI/UX Design Interactive Prototyping Design Experiment

Concept & Object Choice

This design experiment explores Attar (Ittar), an alcohol-free, oil-based perfume deeply rooted in Middle Eastern and South Asian culture. Selected as an object rich in physical exchange, status, and sensory associations, the goal of the digital app concept is to allow users to build a custom scent profile based on individual ingredients or desired emotional moods.

Design Process & Iteration

To address the challenge of digital medium lacking a sense of smell, sensory associations were translated into visual and textual cues. Using an idea map, aromatic ingredients were categorized into three core groups: Flowers, Spices, and Wood. The initial wireframe explored a simple layout with top navigation and a central liquid container.

Elements like typography, icons, and buttons were updated with warm, carved wood and polished metallic textures to evoke an authentic, artisanal luxury feel. Sliders and conflicting taste spectrums (e.g., Spicy vs. Sweet, Floral vs. Woody) were integrated to give users granular, real-time control over their custom fragrance mix.

1. Visual Moodboard 1: Initial exploration of visual characteristics, raw botanical ingredients, and cultural context surrounding Attar.
2. Tactile & Emotional Idea Map: Mapping physical tactile traits, aromatic note categories, and cultural/emotional associations of fragrance exchange.
3. Visual Moodboard 2: Warm wood, agarwood, and earth tone color palette established for the final UI theme.
4. Low-Fidelity Wireframe: Early structural concept laying out the category tabs, liquid vessel display, and metric indicators.
5. Final Interactive UI Mockup: State Shown: Adjusting Wood notes. Before: User navigated from the Floral and Spice tabs to select Wood ingredients. After: User taps the arrows to inspect bottle angles or selects "Name of Your Attar" to save their custom mix.

MEDE 3500 · Advanced Video Production

OCF Juniors Lead Climbing — Video Documentary

Documentary Production Cinematography Sports Journalism

Project Brief & Concept

I produced a 5-minute short documentary covering the Ontario Climbing Federation (OCF) Junior Lead Climbing Provincial Championship hosted at The Hub Mississauga.

Production & Narrative Direction

I pre-planned my production equipment and shot list to navigate the fast-paced, live competition environment. On location, I operated a tripod-mounted Nikon DSLR, executing manual pan and zoom tracking to follow athletes along high-wall lead routes. I captured crisp audio using a lapel mic for on-site interviews alongside a shotgun mic for ambient gym and crowd reactions. In post-production, I edited the film in Adobe Premiere Pro, balancing dual-audio tracks, pacing the narrative, and colour-grading indoor lighting to produce a polished final edit.

Short Documentary Film (5:10): Directed, shot, and edited for MEDE 3500. Capturing the Ontario Provincial Lead Climbing Championships at The Hub Mississauga.

Synergy Digital — B2B Strategy & Brand Plan

Entrepreneurship Brand Strategy Financial Compliance Financial Modeling

Business Concept & Value Proposition

Developed as a comprehensive business plan for DCOM 3900, Synergy Digital is a specialized freelance digital communications consultancy serving regulated financial professionals, wealth managers, and fintech startups in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). The business bridges the gap between high-cost generalist agencies lacking compliance knowledge and niche consultants who lack creative execution.

Market Positioning & Growth Strategy

Operating under a compliance-first philosophy, Synergy Digital integrates CIRO and OSC regulatory standards into the creative process from day one. The service model introduces recurring revenue through monthly Thought Leadership Retainers ($3,500–$8,000/mo), paired with project-based Compliance Content Audits and Investor Education Funnels to convert educational content into qualified leads.
